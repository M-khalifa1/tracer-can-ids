# AI Assistance and Human Validation

Generative AI tools, including ChatGPT, were used as supporting tools during this research project.

AI assistance included:

- Technical brainstorming and development guidance.
- Debugging and code-review support.
- Experimental planning and evaluation guidance.
- Documentation organization and language refinement.
- Assistance with research writing and presentation.

All AI-assisted outputs were critically reviewed by the researchers.

The implementation, experimental procedures, reported results, and scientific interpretations remain the responsibility of the authors and were evaluated against their technical expertise and available experimental evidence.

AI-generated suggestions were not treated as independent experimental evidence.

The authors retain full responsibility for the scientific accuracy, integrity, and conclusions of this work.
